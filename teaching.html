Teaching & education

Courses, notes and material

Teaching here follows the same logic as the research: start from a mechanism, make the model do real work, and let students break things in the lab.

Courses taught

Courses

BSc, MSc and PhD courses in the Bioengineering Department at KFUPM.

  • BioE 505 · PhD Modeling of Biological Systems: turning a physical description of a tissue, a flow or a mechanism into equations that can be validated against measurement. Also taught as LS 504 for MSc and PhD students.
  • BioE 524 · MSc / PhD Mechanics of Biological Materials: structure–property relationships in biological materials, and what they imply for architectured design.
  • BioE 480 · BSc Biomechanics: how bone, soft tissue and joints carry load, and how that behaviour is measured and modelled.
  • BioE 455 · BSc Transport Phenomena: momentum, heat and mass transport for bioengineers, from the conservation laws through to transport in tissue and in devices.
  • BioE 370 · BSc Bioengineering Thermodynamics: classical and statistical thermodynamics applied to biological and biomedical systems.
  • BioE 355 · BSc Biomimicry: moving from a biological observation to an engineering specification without stopping at the metaphor.
  • BioE 316 · BSc Bioethics: consent, risk, animal use and data: the questions the Ethics node carries in the research map.
  • Earlier · NYU Abu Dhabi and elsewhere Partial Differential Equations for Engineering: taught at NYU Abu Dhabi before KFUPM, alongside earlier teaching in deformable solids, finite element analysis, fluid mechanics and heat transfer.
