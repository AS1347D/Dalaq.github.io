Bioinspired mechanicsRunning

Artificial lung for direct air carbon capture

A gas-exchange device that borrows the flow architecture of the hummingbird lung, where air moves in one direction through a dense network rather than in and out of dead-ended sacs, and applies it to capturing carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

Since
2025
Role
Co-Investigator
Funder
Research, Development and Innovation Authority (RDIA), Saudi Arabia

Architected materialsCompleted

Mechanics of segmented materials

Segmented and topologically interlocked assemblies (spine-like columns, tetrahedra and octahedra blocks) engineered so that strength and toughness rise together instead of trading against one another.

Period
2025–2026
Role
Principal Investigator
Funder

Bioinspired segmented structures

Adaptive systemsRunning

Origami cartridges for biotic monitoring

Folded cartridges that deploy, sample and reset on their own, so biological monitoring can run unattended in the field. Built on the group's Kresling-spring work, where the fold pattern rather than a motor supplies the motion.

Since
2025
Role
Principal Investigator
Funder
KFUPM IRC for Biosystems and Machines

Adaptive systemsProposed

Kresling origami adapter for endovascular surgery

A folded adapter that gives a catheter tip extra flexibility and a grasping motion inside a vessel, driven by the coupled twist and extension of a Kresling cell rather than by added mechanism.

Status

Sensitivity mapping of the ear canal

EthicsRunning

Ethicalc: ethical matrix analysis

A standalone MATLAB application that post-processes Ethical Matrix analyses, triaging the tensions between stakeholders and principles into colour-coded matrices without collapsing them into a single score. Next: a database of ethical dilemmas, each case stored in matrix form.
