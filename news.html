Updates

News

  • 2026–2027 Conference service. Ahmed S. Dalaq is a Technical Program Chair for BioBIS 2027, the Advances in Bioengineering and Biomedical Conference hosted by KFUPM in March 2027.
  • 2026 Appointment. Ahmed S. Dalaq becomes Acting Director of the Interdisciplinary Research Center for Biosystems and Machines at KFUPM. Meet the group
  • 2026 New grant. A project with the IRC for Biosystems and Machines to rebuild transtibial prosthetic design around digitalisation and generative design. Project
  • 2026 New paper. An origami-inspired unit cell composed of intersecting Kresling springs is out in Engineering Structures. DOI
  • 2026 New paper. On the origins of toughness in Corymbia calophylla (Marri tree) nuts, in Advanced Science. DOI
  • 2026 Ethics. A colour-coded triage method for the ethical matrix is published in the Journal of Agricultural and Environmental Ethics. DOI
  • 2025 Patent filed. A United States provisional application on the artificial lung for direct air carbon capture, now moving to a non-provisional filing. Patents
  • 2025 National grant. Funding from Saudi Arabia's Research, Development and Innovation Authority for the artificial lung project, with the group as Co-Investigator. Project
  • 2025 Two new projects. Sensitivity mapping of the ear canal and origami-based cartridges for automatic biotic monitoring both start at the IRC for Biosystems and Machines. Running projects
  • 2025 Talk. Nonfolding origami lattice for energy dissipation and strength, presented at the 12th European Solid Mechanics Conference in Lyon.
  • 2024 Award. Outstanding department service, Bioengineering Department, KFUPM (2023–2024).
  • 2023 Session chair. Ahmed S. Dalaq chaired a session at PowerMEMS 2023 in Abu Dhabi, where the group also presented on AI-based shape optimisation of galloping micro-power generators.
  • Standing Open positions. PhD and postdoctoral opportunities are open on a rolling basis. Details
