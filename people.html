The group

A laboratory inside KFUPM's Interdisciplinary Research Center for Biosystems and Machines, working across mechanics, materials and bioengineering.

Group head

Dr. Ahmed S. Dalaq

Acting Director, IRC for Biosystems and Machines
Assistant Professor, Bioengineering Department, KFUPM

The laboratory applies origami principles and bioinspiration to design smart materials and adaptive structures across scales, alongside a line of work on ear-canal discomfort and the design of limb prostheses, ear prostheses and earplugs.

He holds a PhD in mechanical engineering from McGill University, an MSc from the Masdar Institute of Science and Technology and a BSc from the American University of Sharjah, and held postdoctoral appointments at NYU Abu Dhabi and in Montreal before joining KFUPM in 2023.

Technical Program Chair for BioBIS 2027 at KFUPM, and session chair at PowerMEMS 2023.

  • Applied mechanics
  • Architectured materials
  • Origami metamaterials
  • Additive manufacturing
  • Biomechanics
  • Biomimetics

Opportunities

We look for people who are comfortable moving between a model, a testing machine and a conversation with a clinician or a biologist. Prior experience in every pillar is not expected; curiosity across them is.

Doctoral

PhD opportunity

Doctoral positions in bioinspired mechanics, architected and interlocked materials, origami-based structures, computational mechanics and biomedical devices. Applicants should have a strong background in mechanics or a closely related engineering discipline.

  • Say which running project or theme interests you and why
  • Attach a CV and transcripts
  • Mention a paper of ours you have read

Email about a PhD

Postdoctoral

Postdoc opportunity

Postdoctoral positions for researchers who can lead a line of work: experimental mechanics, large-scale simulation, or the translation of a result into a device. Fellowship applications made together with the group are welcome.

  • Outline the line of work you would lead here
  • Attach a CV with a publication list
  • Note any fellowship you intend to apply for
